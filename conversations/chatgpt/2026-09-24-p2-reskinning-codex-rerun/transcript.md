On Apple-silicon MacBooks, including your MacBook Pro, the **built-in microphone is hardware-disconnected when the lid is fully closed**. That is intentional, so macOS/apps cannot use the internal mic while the Mac is in clamshell mode.

That explains exactly what you just observed: with the lid open, the internal microphone registers; with the lid shut, it stops.

If you want to use the Mac closed with an external monitor, you’ll need an external microphone such as:
- AirPods or another Bluetooth headset
- a USB microphone
- a webcam with a microphone
- a monitor with a built-in microphone
